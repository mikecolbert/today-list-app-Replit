# Section 1: Product Overview

## App Name
Today List

## One-Sentence Description
A lightweight daily logging app that helps busy professionals capture what actually happened each day and turn it into a searchable, emotional timeline of their life.

## Problem It Solves
People often struggle to remember when meaningful events happened—finishing a major work project, taking a family trip, or even small but memorable moments from daily life. This information is rarely captured in one place and is difficult to reconstruct later from calendars, emails, or memory alone. As a result, important moments are forgotten, timelines become unclear, and both personal reflection and professional recall become frustrating and time-consuming.

## Target Users
Busy professionals with families who:
- Naturally reflect on their day in the evening  
- Want a simple, low-effort way to record daily life  
- Need to recall past events for work, family, or personal reasons  
- Value both productivity and meaningful life moments  

## Core Value Proposition
Today List makes it effortless to build a nightly habit of capturing your day in just one or two sentences. Over time, it transforms these entries into a structured, searchable record of your life—organized by categories like work, family, and personal—so you can quickly find specific moments, review recent weeks or months, and reconnect with meaningful experiences.

## Key Differentiator
Unlike calendars or task managers that focus on what was planned, Today List captures what actually happened—turning everyday moments into a structured, searchable life history.

## Product Philosophy
- Nightly Ritual: Designed around a simple end-of-day habit (e.g., 9 p.m. reflection) that fits naturally into users’ lives  
- Low Friction: Entries are intentionally limited to one or two sentences to encourage consistency and reduce effort  
- Capture Over Complexity: Focused on quickly recording real events, not managing tasks or schedules  
- Searchable Life Timeline: Every entry is automatically timestamped and easily retrievable by keyword, category, or time range  
- Emotionally Meaningful: Combines practical recall (e.g., work history) with emotional reflection (e.g., family memories and personal milestones)  

## How It Works (Conceptual)
Users spend less than a minute each evening writing a short summary of their day and selecting a category (work, family, personal). Over time, these entries become a chronological timeline that can be:
- Searched by keyword (e.g., “Disneyland”)  
- Filtered by category (e.g., work)  
- Reviewed by time period (e.g., last week, last month)  

## Product Framing (Tagline Options)
- “Remember your life, not just your schedule.”  
- “A timeline of what actually happened.”  
- “Your life, searchable.”
